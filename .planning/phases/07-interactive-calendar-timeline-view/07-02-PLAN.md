<threat_model>
ASVS Level: 1
Threat Analysis:
- Daylight Saving Time / Leap Day Desynchronization: Week view column alignment shifting or missing days on daylight saving transitions.
- CPU Overload from 7 Parallel Grids: Redundant recalculations of all 7 days on every timer tick.
Mitigation:
- Anchor week calculation to `calendar.date(from: calendar.dateComponents([.yearForWeekOfYear, .weekOfYear], from: baseDate))` with exact 7-day stride.
- Cache positioned blocks per day column, recalculating only when `store.timeEntries` changes.
</threat_model>
